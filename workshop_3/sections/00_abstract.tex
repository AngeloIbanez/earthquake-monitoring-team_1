\begin{abstract}
Reliable earthquake monitoring requires clean, unified multi-agency telemetry and clear analytical summaries that separate routine background tremors from dangerous seismic anomalies. Building on the containerized Bronze and Silver pipeline from Workshop~\#2, this report documents the improvements made to our ingestion and curation modules alongside the new Silver-to-Gold analytical layer built for Workshop~\#3. We expanded ingestion from a single USGS feed to three global agencies (USGS, EMSC, and GDACS), replaced silent zero-fill fallbacks with strict validation and quarantine storage, added incremental watermark tracking with overlap windows, linked duplicate cross-agency reports into unified event groups, and resolved both Parquet timestamp and container dependency incompatibilities. Orchestrated by Apache Airflow inside a custom OpenJDK~17 Docker image, our PySpark Gold pipeline reads all accumulated Silver Parquet files (4,786 raw rows, 3,806 unique earthquakes) and outputs two Gold Parquet summaries: 113 data governance KPIs and 84 storytelling and Seismic Activity Anomaly Index (SAAI) aggregations, alongside 637 reconciled multi-agency groups (1,274 events) in PostgreSQL.
\end{abstract}
